What is LIONPLAY?

LIONPLAY is a media player that connects to IPTV services you already subscribe to. It ships with no channels or content — you add your own provider (M3U playlist, Xtream Codes, or Stalker/MAG portal).

Getting started

  1. Open the App and tap “Add Server”.
  2. Choose your provider type (M3U, Xtream, or Stalker).
  3. Enter the address and credentials given to you by your IPTV subscription.
  4. Your channels, movies and series load automatically.

Frequently asked

The App is empty / no channels. LIONPLAY does not include content. You must add your own IPTV provider details first.

A stream won’t play. Check that your subscription is active and the server address is correct. Some servers are HTTP-only; the App supports these.

How do I back up my servers? Settings → iCloud Backup. Backups are stored in your own iCloud account.

How do I remove my data? Delete the App, or clear servers/history from Settings. No data is stored on our servers.
