## Hi there 👋

# LionPlay 🦁  👋

**LionPlay is a “bring your own content” IPTV / media player for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV.**

> ⚠️ **We are NOT a content provider.** LionPlay does **not** sell, provide, host, or
> aggregate any channels, streams, movies, series, or subscriptions. The app ships
> **empty** — you connect **your own** IPTV service that you already obtained and are
> legally entitled to use. You alone are responsible for the content you add.

---

### What it does
- Plays IPTV from sources **you** add: **M3U playlist**, **Xtream Codes**, or **Stalker / MAG portal**
- Live TV, movies & series with EPG, favorites, watch history
- Chromecast & AirPlay, PiP, subtitles, parental lock, iCloud backup
- No ads. No preloaded content. Bring your own provider.
