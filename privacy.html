LIONPLAY Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 June 2026

LIONPLAY (“the App”) is a “bring your own provider” IPTV / media player. The App does not provide, host, or aggregate any video content. You connect the App to IPTV services you already subscribe to (M3U, Xtream Codes, or Stalker/MAG portal) and the App plays the streams you are entitled to.

1. Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our own servers. The App has no developer-operated backend, no advertising, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

2. Data stored on your device

  • IPTV server credentials (server address, username/password, MAC) are stored locally on your device. Passwords are kept in the iOS Keychain.
  • Preferences, favorites, and watch history are stored locally.
  • If you enable iCloud Backup, this data is synced through your own Apple iCloud account (Apple’s CloudKit). It is never accessible to the developer. See Apple’s privacy policy for iCloud handling.

3. Third-party connections you choose to use

  • Your IPTV provider: the App sends your credentials and playback requests directly to the server you enter. We are not affiliated with these providers; their data handling is governed by their own policies.
  • Trakt (optional): if you sign in to Trakt, your viewing activity is sent to Trakt at your request, governed by Trakt’s privacy policy. You can disconnect at any time in Settings.
  • Metadata/artwork (optional): the App may fetch movie/series artwork and descriptions from public metadata sources (e.g., TMDB) to enrich the interface. No personal data is sent.

4. Location

If you enable the optional weather widget, the App uses your approximate location to show local weather. This is processed on-device and is not transmitted to the developer.

5. Permissions

  • Local Network: to discover and cast to Chromecast devices.
  • Notifications: to show EPG program reminders you set.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: to unlock the optional parental lock.
  • Location (optional): weather widget only.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children. An optional PIN-protected parental lock is provided.

7. Data deletion

All data is on your device. Deleting the App removes all local data. You can also clear servers, history, and caches from within Settings. The App does not create a developer account, so there is no remote account to delete.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change accordingly.
